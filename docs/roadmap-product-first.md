# Spark AI Product-First Roadmap

## Roadmap principle

Every sprint must create a business asset that improves product clarity, enterprise trust, technical credibility, partner readiness or conversion. Engineering optimization is enabling work, not a standalone roadmap priority.

Design Tokens, Lighthouse, WCAG, asset migration, component cleanup and infrastructure work enter a sprint only when they are required to ship or validate the active product milestone.

## Priority 0 — RTX Spark Appliance™ Product Definition

### Business question

What exactly is RTX Spark Appliance™, who is it for, what problem does it solve, and why should an enterprise buy it instead of assembling separate AI compute, storage, RAG and archival systems?

### Required product assets

- Canonical product category and one-sentence definition
- Primary customer profiles and buying committee
- Priority enterprise problems and trigger events
- Product architecture and system boundary
- Core capabilities and defensible differentiation
- Deployment, governance and data-sovereignty model
- Product configurations and commercial packaging assumptions
- Proof requirements, limitations and claims policy
- Approved Chinese and English messaging hierarchy
- Product FAQ and objection handling

### Exit criteria

One approved Product Definition Document becomes the source of truth for all product, architecture, industry, investor and sales materials.

## Priority 1 — World-Class Product Page

### Business question

Why does RTX Spark Appliance™ exist, how does it work, and what enterprise outcome justifies a demo or procurement conversation?

### Required product assets

- Investor-grade hero and clear category definition
- Business outcome narrative
- Product system visual and real product imagery
- Capabilities, architecture and deployment sections
- Governance, security and archival trust sections
- Proof, specifications, FAQ and conversion paths
- Product brief and demo CTAs
- Chinese and English parity

### Exit criteria

The page enables an enterprise buyer, ODM partner or investor to understand the product, its differentiation and the next action without external explanation.

## Priority 2 — Enterprise Architecture Page

### Business question

How does the platform fit into an enterprise environment and maintain control across data, models, agents, storage and long-term retention?

### Required product assets

- Layered reference architecture
- Data and knowledge lifecycle
- Integration boundaries and interfaces
- Security, identity, policy and audit model
- Deployment patterns
- Availability and operational responsibility model
- Architecture FAQ and downloadable diagram

### Exit criteria

Technical decision-makers can evaluate system fit, governance boundaries and integration implications.

## Priority 3 — Industry Solution Pages

### Business question

How does the same platform create industry-specific value under real regulatory, operational and data constraints?

### Delivery order

1. Healthcare
2. Government
3. Finance
4. Manufacturing
5. Education
6. Media

### Required product assets per industry

- Industry problem and trigger event
- Priority workflows
- Data types and retention requirements
- Architecture pattern
- Governance and compliance considerations
- Business outcomes and proof plan
- Industry-specific CTA

### Exit criteria

Each page presents a credible solution narrative rather than a generic vertical reskin.

## Priority 4 — Product Brief Download Center

### Business question

What can a buyer, partner or stakeholder take away and circulate internally after visiting the website?

### Required product assets

- Product brief
- Technical overview
- Architecture overview
- Industry briefs
- Governance and archival brief
- Versioning, language and document metadata
- Gated and ungated download strategy

### Exit criteria

Every downloadable asset has a defined audience, owner, version and conversion purpose.

## Priority 5 — Investor Landing Page

### Business question

Why is Spark AI positioned to build a significant enterprise AI infrastructure company now?

### Required product assets

- Company thesis and market timing
- Product and platform wedge
- Defensibility and expansion model
- Market and customer evidence
- Business model and partnership strategy
- Milestones and investor CTA

### Exit criteria

The investor page connects market opportunity, product differentiation and company execution into one credible investment narrative.

## Engineering enablement policy

Engineering work must be attached to one of the milestones above.

| Engineering activity | Entry condition |
| --- | --- |
| Design Tokens | Required to deliver visual consistency across the active milestone |
| Lighthouse | Required before milestone release or when performance blocks conversion |
| WCAG | Required for each shipped customer-facing surface |
| Asset migration | Required when a milestone uses or replaces affected assets |
| Component refactoring | Required when at least two active product surfaces share the pattern |
| SEO implementation | Required when a product or solution page is ready for indexing |
| Analytics | Required when a milestone introduces a measurable conversion action |

## Recommended sprint sequence

- Sprint 06: RTX Spark Appliance™ Product Definition
- Sprint 07: Product Page Information Architecture and Core Narrative
- Sprint 08: Product Page Visual System and Product Storytelling
- Sprint 09: Product Page Build, QA and Conversion Release
- Sprint 10: Enterprise Architecture Definition and Page
- Sprint 11–13: Priority Industry Solution Pages
- Sprint 14: Product Brief Download Center
- Sprint 15: Investor Landing Page

Sequence may change based on validated customer evidence, commercial deadlines or product readiness. A sprint should not advance because a page is visually complete if its underlying product claims remain unresolved.
