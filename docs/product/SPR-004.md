# SPR-004 — Product Roadmap

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Product Leadership

## Priority sequence

0. Spark Appliance™ Product Definition
1. World-class Product Page
2. Enterprise Architecture Page
3. Industry Solution Pages
4. Product Brief Download Center
5. Investor Landing Page

## Operating principle

Engineering optimization supports product milestones. It does not precede them as an isolated roadmap objective.

## Roadmap gates

- Product definition before final product-page claims
- Architecture approval before technical storytelling
- Claims evidence before quantitative promotion
- Industry specificity before vertical page release
- Download ownership and versioning before public distribution
- Product and market evidence before investor amplification

## Sprint relationship

Each sprint must deliver a business asset that improves product clarity, trust, technical credibility, enterprise readiness or conversion.
