# SPR-001 — Spark Appliance™ PRD

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Product

## Product definition

Spark Appliance™ is the flagship Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance for Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure.

It is intended to unify accelerated AI compute, Intelligent Tiered Storage, Enterprise RAG, AI Agent capabilities, data governance and long-term Blu-ray archival in one enterprise platform.

## Product outcome

Turn long-retained enterprise data into governed, attributable and reusable AI knowledge without requiring customers to assemble unrelated infrastructure stacks.

## Primary customers

- Regulated enterprises
- Government and public sector
- Healthcare
- Finance
- Manufacturing and engineering
- Knowledge-intensive institutions
