# SR-IDX-001 — Research Domain Index

**Domain:** SR Research  
**Status:** Active

## Mandate

SR develops evidence, frameworks and original thinking that improve Spark AI product, engineering and market decisions.

## Scope

- Enterprise AI infrastructure research
- Cold-data economics and lifecycle research
- Retrieval, agent and knowledge-system evaluation
- Storage and archival research
- Industry and regulatory research
- Experiments and benchmark methodology
- White papers
- Evidence reviews and literature synthesis

## Current source documents

- [SPK-WP-001 — From Cold Data to Governed AI Knowledge](../whitepapers/SPK-WP-001.md)

## Evidence rule

Research findings are not automatically product specifications or marketing claims. Adoption requires review by the relevant Product, Engineering and Brand owners.

## Future document families

- `SR-WP-*` White papers
- `SR-RPT-*` Research reports
- `SR-EXP-*` Experiment records
- `SR-BMK-*` Benchmark methods and results
- `SR-LIT-*` Literature reviews
- `SR-HYP-*` Research hypotheses
