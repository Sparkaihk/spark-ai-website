# SR-002 — AI Knowledge Infrastructure

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Research and Product Strategy

## Research thesis

AI Knowledge Infrastructure is the system layer that converts enterprise data into governed, attributable and operationally reusable knowledge for people, applications and AI Agents.

## Research dimensions

- Data quality and provenance
- Retrieval quality and attribution
- Knowledge lifecycle
- Identity and policy propagation
- Agent tool and action governance
- Storage and compute locality
- Long-term evidence preservation
- Evaluation and observability

## Core distinction

AI Knowledge Infrastructure is broader than a vector database, RAG application or model-serving stack. It connects the full lifecycle from retained source data to governed knowledge use and durable evidence.
