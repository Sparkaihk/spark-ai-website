# SR-001 — White Paper

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Research

## Working title

From Cold Data to Governed AI Knowledge

## Research question

How should enterprises combine compute, storage, retrieval, governance, AI Agent capabilities and archival to activate long-retained information without losing attribution or control?

## Required sections

- Cold-data value thesis
- Failure modes of fragmented AI stacks
- Governed data-to-knowledge lifecycle
- Reference architecture
- Integrated appliance rationale
- Enterprise evaluation framework
- Business outcomes
- Claims and limitations

## Evidence requirements

Separate architectural reasoning, research evidence, product capability and commercial claim. Publication requires Product, Engineering and Brand review.
