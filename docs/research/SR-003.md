# SR-003 — Industry Research

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Research and Industry Product Strategy

## Priority industries

1. Healthcare
2. Government
3. Finance
4. Manufacturing
5. Education
6. Media

## Research template

For each industry, document:

- Priority business problems
- Buyer and stakeholder map
- Data types and formats
- Retention and governance requirements
- AI knowledge workflows
- Deployment constraints
- Existing alternatives
- Architecture pattern
- Business outcomes
- Evidence and proof plan
- Claims and regulatory limitations

## Release rule

Industry solution pages must be based on documented workflows and constraints. Generic vertical reskins do not satisfy this research standard.
