# DLSM Website and Investor Update Spec

## Goal

Add DLSM to the Spark AI website as the intelligent control plane for AI data lifecycle governance, and make `/investors` a distinct investor-facing page.

## Approved scope

- Preserve the existing homepage hero, route structure, navigation labels, logo, bilingual behavior, and blue-white design system.
- Add one homepage section that explains DLSM in business and architecture terms.
- Replace the investor route's homepage reuse with an independent investor narrative.
- Position DLSM as Spark AI's proposed control plane across AI data objects, logical storage, physical media, lifecycle policy, and audit evidence.
- Describe commercialization through assessment, pilot, deployment, and expansion, without presenting roadmap items as completed capabilities.
- Do not publish unverified market size, revenue, savings, performance, compliance, customer, or standards claims.

## Content boundary

DLSM is not presented as an established unified international product category or an approved industry standard. Sanitization commands are not presented as proof of a verified outcome. Claims about current capability, pilot validation, and future direction must remain distinguishable.

## Acceptance

- Homepage contains a bilingual DLSM business section and a direct investor CTA.
- `/investors` no longer renders the homepage component.
- Investor page explains the problem, control-plane architecture, participation model, and pilot route.
- No em dash appears in newly added visible copy.
- Type checking and production build pass.
- Homepage and investor page receive rendered desktop and mobile inspection.
