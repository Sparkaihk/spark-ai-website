# Spark AI Brand Architecture

Status: Canonical naming source for website, product, technical, sales and investor content.

## Approved architecture

| Level | Approved name |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Spark AI Technology Limited |
| Brand | Spark AI™ |
| Platform | Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure |
| Flagship product | Spark Appliance™ |

## Technology layer

The following terms describe technology capabilities. They are not product names:

- Powered by NVIDIA GPU
- Enterprise RAG
- Intelligent Tiered Storage
- Blu-ray Archive
- AI Agent

## Naming rules

### Required

- Use **Spark Appliance™** for the flagship product in headlines, navigation, CTA labels, product cards, diagrams, captions, metadata and downloadable collateral.
- Use **Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure** for the platform layer.
- Use **Spark AI™** for the brand.
- Use **Spark AI Technology Limited** for the legal company name.

### Prohibited

- Do not use `RTX Spark Appliance™`.
- Do not use `RTX Spark Appliance`.
- Do not use `NVIDIA RTX Spark` as a product name.
- Do not place NVIDIA branding in marketing headlines, navigation labels, CTA labels or primary product identity.

### NVIDIA reference boundary

NVIDIA may appear only in clearly labeled technical specification or technology sections where the reference is accurate and supportable.

Approved construction:

**Powered by NVIDIA GPU**

Avoid implying NVIDIA ownership, endorsement, certification or product naming unless documented authorization exists. NVIDIA marks should follow the applicable trademark and partner-brand guidelines.

## Messaging hierarchy

### Product label

Spark Appliance™

### Category

Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance

### Platform relationship

Spark Appliance™ is the flagship system for Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure.

### Canonical short definition

Spark Appliance™ unifies accelerated AI compute, intelligent tiered storage, Enterprise RAG, AI Agent capabilities, governance and long-term Blu-ray archival in one enterprise platform.

### CTA language

- Explore Spark Appliance™
- Request a Demo
- Download Product Brief
- Talk to an Architect

Do not use `Explore RTX Spark Appliance`, `Book an RTX Spark Demo` or similar legacy constructions.

## SEO rules

- Product page title begins with `Spark Appliance™`.
- Product page descriptions identify Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure and the Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance category.
- Legacy naming must not remain in page titles, Open Graph data, structured data, image alt text or generated social cards.
- If legacy search demand is material, address it through controlled redirect or migration metadata rather than retaining the old name in visible product identity.

## Image and diagram rules

- Image alt text and captions use `Spark Appliance™`.
- Architecture diagrams label the product boundary `Spark Appliance™`.
- NVIDIA appears only in an internal technology/specification layer, never as the diagram title.
- Existing raster artwork containing the legacy name must be regenerated or edited; alt text changes alone are insufficient.

## Download rules

- Product brief title: `Spark Appliance™ Product Brief`.
- Public filename: `spark-appliance-product-brief-en.pdf` or language equivalent.
- PDF title, subject, author metadata, internal headings and image captions must use the approved architecture.
- Legacy download URLs should redirect when filenames change.

## Migration acceptance test

The migration is complete only when repository and rendered-site searches confirm:

1. Zero public references to `RTX Spark Appliance™`.
2. Zero product-name uses of `NVIDIA RTX Spark`.
3. NVIDIA appears only in technical specification contexts.
4. Hero, navigation, CTA, product pages, diagrams, metadata, downloads and captions use `Spark Appliance™`.
5. Chinese and English surfaces use the same brand architecture.
6. Legacy URLs have intentional redirect or compatibility handling.
