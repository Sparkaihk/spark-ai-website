# SES-IDX-001 — Engineering Domain Index

**Domain:** SES Engineering  
**Status:** Active

## Mandate

SES defines how Spark AI products and platforms are designed, implemented, secured, operated and verified.

## Scope

- Reference architecture
- Hardware and software specifications
- Architecture decision records
- Security and data-governance implementation
- APIs and integration contracts
- Reliability and operational standards
- Performance methodology
- Development and release engineering
- Technical accessibility and quality standards

## Required interfaces

- Receives product requirements and priorities from SPR.
- Provides feasibility, architecture boundaries and verified specifications to SPR.
- Provides approved technical terminology and limitations to SB.
- Consumes research findings from SR after technical review.
- Escalates material security, legal or operational risk to SC.

## Future document families

- `SES-ADR-*` Architecture decisions
- `SES-SPEC-*` Technical specifications
- `SES-SEC-*` Security standards
- `SES-SOP-*` Engineering procedures
- `SES-RPT-*` Verification and performance reports
- `SES-RFC-*` Proposed technical changes
