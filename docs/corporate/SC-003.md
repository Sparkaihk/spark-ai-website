# SC-003 — Company Handbook

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Corporate Operations

## Purpose

Translate Spark AI’s Constitution and Governance system into daily operating expectations.

## Handbook structure

- Company mission and doctrine
- Organization and responsibilities
- Foundation System usage
- Decision and approval processes
- Product-development lifecycle
- Engineering and security practices
- Brand and communication review
- Research and evidence standards
- Customer and partner conduct
- Confidentiality and information handling
- Risk escalation
- Document retention and knowledge transfer

## Operating expectations

- Use current source documents.
- Record material decisions and assumptions.
- Do not present roadmap items as current capability.
- Escalate unsupported claims.
- Preserve customer confidentiality and data boundaries.
- Prefer clear ownership over informal consensus.
- Improve reusable institutional knowledge after every sprint.
