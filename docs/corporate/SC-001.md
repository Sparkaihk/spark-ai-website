# SC-001 — Constitution

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Company Leadership

## Purpose

Define the durable principles governing Spark AI Technology Limited.

## Constitutional principles

- Build technology that converts enterprise data into governed knowledge.
- Preserve customer control, attribution and long-term institutional memory.
- Prefer evidence over amplification.
- Make architecture and responsibility explainable.
- Treat security, governance and operational trust as product responsibilities.
- Align engineering investment with customer and company value.
- Distinguish research hypotheses, product commitments and public claims.

## Authority

Corporate policy, product definition, engineering specification and brand expression must remain traceable to an accountable owner and current source document.

## Related doctrine

`SPK-000 — Spark AI Doctrine` remains a foundational source until reissued under this Constitution.
