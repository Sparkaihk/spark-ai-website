# SC-002 — Governance

**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Company Leadership

## Purpose

Define decision ownership, approval authority and review obligations across the Foundation System.

## Domain ownership

- SB owns brand expression.
- SES owns implementation truth and technical verification.
- SPR owns product definition and priority.
- SR owns research methods and evidence development.
- SC owns company policy, risk and institutional accountability.

## Decision rules

- Product claims require SPR ownership and evidence status.
- Technical claims require SES verification.
- Public expression requires SB review.
- Material legal, financial, partnership or reputation risk requires SC review.
- Research does not become a public product claim without adoption by the relevant owners.

## Governance records

- Decision log
- Claims register
- Risk register
- Document registry
- Approval record
- Supersession and archive record
