# Brand Architecture

## Company

**Spark AI Technology Limited** is the legal company name. Use it in corporate, legal, investor, contractual and formal contact contexts.

## Brand

**Spark AI™** is the customer-facing brand. It represents the company’s enterprise AI infrastructure portfolio and should lead general market communication.

## Platform

**Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure** is the platform layer connecting enterprise data, governed knowledge, AI runtime and durable information infrastructure.

Recommended relationship statement:

> Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure turns long-lived enterprise data into governed, reusable AI knowledge.

## Flagship product

**Spark Appliance™** is the flagship product.

Recommended relationship statement:

> Spark Appliance™ is the flagship system for Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure.

## Product category

**Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance** describes the product category. Use it to help buyers understand the product; do not substitute it for the product name.

## Technology layer

The technology layer supports the product proposition:

- Powered by NVIDIA GPU
- Enterprise RAG
- Intelligent Tiered Storage
- Blu-ray Archive
- AI Agent

Supplier or technology names must remain subordinate to Spark Appliance™.

## Required hierarchy

1. Business outcome
2. Spark Appliance™
3. Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure
4. Product category
5. Technology capabilities
6. Supplier details in technical contexts

Do not lead a Hero, navigation item, CTA or product title with a supplier technology name.
