# SB-IDX-001 — Brand Domain Index

**Domain:** SB Brand  
**Status:** Active

## Mandate

SB controls how Spark AI identities, products, claims and ideas are expressed publicly and internally.

## Scope

- Brand architecture
- Naming and trademarks
- Terminology
- Voice and tone
- Visual identity and design system
- Public claims expression
- Website and collateral review
- Partner and supplier branding boundaries
