# SB-002 — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Brand

## Identity rules

- Use Spark AI™ for the brand.
- Use Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure for the platform.
- Use Spark Appliance™ for the flagship product.
- Keep NVIDIA references inside validated technical contexts.
- Do not use legacy `RTX Spark` naming as visible product identity.

## Voice

Clear, assured, technically credible and commercially relevant.

Lead with business outcomes, explain the architecture, qualify claims and avoid unsupported superlatives.

## Required review surfaces

- Navigation and Footer
- Hero and CTA
- Product and architecture pages
- Metadata, SEO and social cards
- Diagrams, captions and alt text
- Product briefs and downloads
- Investor and partner materials
