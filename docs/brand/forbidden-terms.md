# Forbidden Terms

## Prohibited product names

Do not use these as visible product identity:

- RTX Spark Appliance™
- RTX Spark Appliance
- RTX Spark
- NVIDIA RTX Spark Appliance
- NVIDIA Spark Appliance
- NVIDIA RTX Spark used as the Spark AI product name

Legacy strings may appear only in migration records, compatibility paths or historical references clearly marked as such.

## Prohibited supplier positioning

- Do not lead a product headline with `NVIDIA`.
- Do not label navigation or CTA actions with NVIDIA as the product owner.
- Do not imply NVIDIA endorsement, certification or partnership without documented approval.

## Prohibited generic hype

Avoid:

- revolutionary
- game-changing
- world’s first
- unlimited
- fully autonomous
- guaranteed compliance
- unbreakable security
- zero risk
- instant transformation
- future-proof

These terms may be used only when an approved evidence and legal review process explicitly supports them.

## Prohibited architecture ambiguity

Avoid using these without explanation:

- AI platform
- intelligent infrastructure
- knowledge engine
- sovereign AI
- secure by design
- enterprise-grade

State what the system does, what boundary the term covers and what evidence supports the claim.

## Prohibited claims without evidence

- Exact performance multipliers
- Exact cost reduction
- Capacity limits presented without configuration context
- Archival lifespan without media and environmental assumptions
- Regulatory compliance claims without jurisdiction and control mapping
