# SB-003 — Visual Design System

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Brand Design

## Purpose

Define the visual grammar used to explain Spark AI products, architecture and enterprise outcomes.

## Foundations

- Light enterprise technology palette
- Spark Blue for decisions and governed intelligence
- Cyan reserved for data movement
- Ink hierarchy for credible technical communication
- 12-column desktop grid
- Restrained glass surfaces
- Clear typographic hierarchy
- Motion that explains sequence or causality

## Component families

- HeroSection
- ProductShowcase
- AnimatedDataFlow
- EnterpriseCard
- MetricCard
- CTAButton
- FeatureGrid
- ArchitectureDiagram
- IndustryCard
- SectionHeader
- GlassPanel
- GlowLine
- KnowledgeFlow
- Footer

## Prohibitions

No decorative particles, fake dashboards, generic robots, excessive bloom, unreadable glass effects or animation without product meaning.
