# Naming Rules

## Approved names

| Entity | First reference | Subsequent reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company | Spark AI Technology Limited | Spark AI or the company |
| Brand | Spark AI™ | Spark AI |
| Platform | Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure | the platform |
| Product | Spark Appliance™ | Spark Appliance |
| Category | Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance | the appliance category |

Use the trademark symbol on the first prominent reference per page or document. Avoid repeating it in every paragraph.

## Product naming

Use:

- Spark Appliance™
- Explore Spark Appliance™
- Spark Appliance™ Product Brief
- Spark Appliance™ architecture
- Spark Appliance™ specifications

Do not use NVIDIA branding as the product name.

## NVIDIA references

Approved technical construction:

**Powered by NVIDIA GPU**

NVIDIA may appear in technical specifications, validated workload descriptions and integration documentation. It must not appear as the primary product identity, Hero label, CTA label or navigation label.

## Capitalization

- `Spark AI`, never `Spark Ai` or `SPARK AI` in running copy.
- `Spark Appliance`, title case.
- `Enterprise RAG`, not `Enterprise Rag`.
- `AI Agent` in singular capability references; `AI Agents` for multiple autonomous workflows.
- `Blu-ray`, including the hyphen and lowercase `r`.

## URLs and internal paths

Legacy URLs and asset paths may remain temporarily for compatibility. They are not approved visible names. New public URLs should use `spark-appliance` where practical.

## Bilingual consistency

Do not translate registered or approved product names differently across languages. Keep `Spark Appliance™` intact in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese content.

The Chinese category may be localized, but it must not replace the product name.
