# Voice and Tone

## Brand voice

Spark AI communicates as an enterprise infrastructure company: clear, assured, technically credible and commercially relevant.

## Principles

### Lead with business value

Explain the enterprise outcome before listing technologies.

Prefer:

> Turn long-retained enterprise data into governed AI knowledge.

Avoid:

> GPU, RAG, storage, agents and archive in one box.

### Be precise

Use specific nouns and verifiable claims. Distinguish current capability, configuration-dependent capability and roadmap intent.

### Explain architecture

Connect inputs, processing, governance and outcomes. Do not use technical vocabulary as decoration.

### Respect enterprise risk

Address deployment, control, evidence, security and operational responsibility without overstating compliance.

### Write for the buying committee

- Executives: business value, risk and strategic advantage.
- Technology leaders: architecture, integration and operations.
- Data and AI leaders: knowledge quality, governance and reuse.
- Security and compliance: access, policy, lineage, audit and retention.
- Partners and investors: differentiation, repeatability and expansion potential.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | Visionary but concrete |
| Product page | Confident, explanatory and evidence-led |
| Architecture page | Precise and technically structured |
| Industry page | Domain-aware and outcome-focused |
| Product brief | Concise, portable and procurement-friendly |
| Investor page | Strategic, credible and milestone-oriented |

## Style rules

- Prefer short declarative headlines.
- Use sentence case for headings unless the design system specifies an eyebrow label.
- Avoid more than one major claim per sentence.
- Avoid hype, superlatives and undefined transformation language.
- Keep Chinese and English meaning aligned rather than translating word-for-word.
