# SPK-BG-001 — Spark AI Brand Governance Standard

**Document owner:** Spark AI Technology Limited  
**Status:** Active  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Website, product UI, diagrams, metadata, sales collateral, downloads, investor materials and partner communications

## Purpose

This standard establishes one authoritative brand architecture for Spark AI. It prevents technology suppliers, legacy product names and implementation details from replacing the Spark AI product identity.

## Canonical architecture

- Company: **Spark AI Technology Limited**
- Brand: **Spark AI™**
- Platform: **Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure**
- Flagship product: **Spark Appliance™**
- Product category: **Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance**

## Technology layer

- Powered by NVIDIA GPU
- Enterprise RAG
- Intelligent Tiered Storage
- Blu-ray Archive
- AI Agent

Technology-layer terms describe how the product works. They are not product names.

## Precedence

If website copy, code, diagrams or collateral conflict with this standard, this standard governs unless an approved product decision supersedes it in writing.

## Change control

Changes to the company, brand, platform or flagship product names require approval from product leadership and brand ownership. Technology descriptions may be updated by product and engineering owners when technical evidence changes.
