# Terminology

## Canonical terms

### Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure

The platform connecting governed enterprise data, AI knowledge services, runtime capabilities, storage tiers and long-term archival.

### Spark Appliance™

The flagship Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance for turning underused, long-retained enterprise data into governed AI knowledge.

### Cold data

Long-lived data that is infrequently accessed but remains valuable for knowledge, evidence, compliance, operations or future AI use. Do not imply the data has no value.

### Governed AI knowledge

Knowledge produced from enterprise data with attributable sources, controlled access, policy enforcement, lineage and auditability.

### Enterprise RAG

Retrieval-augmented generation designed for enterprise data, governance, attribution, access control and operational reliability.

### Intelligent Tiered Storage

Policy-driven placement of data across performance, capacity and archival tiers based on access, value, cost and retention requirements.

### Blu-ray Archive

The long-term optical archival layer. Use only when the specific product configuration includes the capability. Avoid unverified lifetime, immutability or compliance claims.

### AI Agent

A governed software capability that uses models, enterprise knowledge and tools to perform controlled tasks. Avoid implying unrestricted autonomy.

### Powered by NVIDIA GPU

A technical capability statement. It does not define the product name and must remain subordinate to Spark Appliance™.

## Claims discipline

Terms including `secure`, `compliant`, `sovereign`, `immutable`, `50+ years`, `1 PB`, `10–100×` and cost-reduction percentages require an evidence source and approved scope.
