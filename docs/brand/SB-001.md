# SB-001 — Brand OS

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Brand and Product Leadership

## Purpose

The Brand OS defines how Spark AI turns company doctrine, product truth and technical evidence into a coherent market identity.

## Core architecture

- Company: Spark AI Technology Limited
- Brand: Spark AI™
- Platform: Spark AI Knowledge Infrastructure
- Flagship product: Spark Appliance™
- Category: Enterprise AI Cold Data Appliance

## Operating layers

1. Doctrine — what Spark AI believes.
2. Product truth — what the product does now.
3. Category — how the market should understand it.
4. Narrative — why the product matters.
5. Expression — language, visual system and motion.
6. Evidence — claims, specifications and proof.
7. Governance — review, approval and change control.

## Brand decision test

Every public expression must improve clarity, trust, technical credibility, enterprise readiness or conversion. Decorative novelty is not a brand objective.
